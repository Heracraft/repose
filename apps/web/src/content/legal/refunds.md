# Refund policy

repose is sold as a monthly plan through Polar, our merchant of record.
This page says when a charge is refunded and how.

## The trial and the first charge

Every plan starts with seven days free. Your card is taken at checkout and
first charged on the eighth day unless you cancel before then; cancelling
during the trial costs nothing.

If you are charged after the trial and change your mind, ask within 14
days of that first charge and it is refunded in full. This applies to the
first charge of a plan only.

## Renewals

A renewal is not refunded for a part of a month. Cancelling stops the next
renewal: the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, your
machines stop then, and their snapshots are kept for 30 days.

## Egress overage

Egress past your plan's allowance is added to the next invoice at $0.05 a
GB. It is a record of traffic your machines sent and is not refunded,
unless a fault on our side caused the traffic.

## Charges in error

A charge that should not have happened (a plan you cancelled in time, a
duplicate, an amount that does not match the published price) is refunded
in full once we have looked at it, whenever it happened.

## How a refund is made

Refunds are made through Polar to the card that paid, and appear on the
same statement, usually within five to ten business days. Ask by email at
the contact address published on repose.herakraft.co, with the email
address of your repose account; a refund cancels the plan it was for.

Polar may also refund a charge on its own, within 60 days of it, to
settle a dispute before it becomes a chargeback; that refund cancels the
plan too. Polar's
[buyer terms](https://polar.sh/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) cover the
payment itself, and Polar answers at support@polar.sh.
