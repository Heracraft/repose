d2-lab (measurer) for the design-2 program (`~/briefs/design2-program-2026-11-26.md`).
Every number here was measured on one Mac, at the UTC times given, with the
instrument named. A number with no run behind it is not in this report.

Three passes, three sessions of d2-lab:

- **D1** (first session, 11:28-15:51Z): today's release on host `e2e-mac2`. Raw data
  `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab/data/`, scripts `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab/bin/`.
- **D2n** (second session, 17:06-19:25Z): the integrated design-2 skeleton,
  `design2` at f9d660b6, on host `e2e-d2n`, after D1 was removed from the Mac. Raw
  data `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab2/data/`, scripts `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab2/bin/`.

Each table has a D1 column, the first D2n column with its verdict, a **D2n final**
column, and the final verdict against D1: **better**, **same** or **worse**, with the
numbers that decide it. "Same" means the ranges overlap and the medians differ by less
than about 15 percent.

## Headline: D2n final against D1

Of the 14 measures, against D1 on the same Mac: six better (2, 3, 8, 10, 11, 12), four
the same (5, 6, 9, 13; 6, 9 and 13 not re-run), one worse (14), two split with a part a
user feels as worse (4 and 7), one not compared (1).

- **Better.** Create (2): warm 12.2-33.9 s, median 16.2 (D1 19.9-23.1, median 20.9).
  Start and stop (3): start to ssh 4.5-5.7 s (D1 11.7-14.9); stop 1.7-2.5 s (D1
  4.0-4.1), with a snapshot 3.7-4.4 s at D1's snapshot size (D1 5.4-6.7); the 61.5 s
  stop tail gone (0 of 55). Memory (8): 0.9 GB per idle machine (D1's appliance 7.9 GB
  with one), 0 at rest and after a stop (D1 7.6 GB after every stop). Snapshots (10):
  create 5.3-11.3 s (D1 16.3-25.4), restore in place 4.9-6.0 s to the file (D1 67-83),
  restore --as 5.1-5.6 s (D1 67-69), +10-17 MB of disk each (D1 ~0.8 GB). Park (11):
  `repose host restart` keeps a machine's memory 3 of 3 and has it back in 44-49 s (D1
  keeps 0 on every restart path, 51-72 s). Host life (12): a killed VM is back on ssh in
  11.9 s by itself (D1 19-25 s); a hostd crash or update leaves the machines running.
- **Same.** ssh and attach (5): new connection 0.76-0.83 s (D1 0.95-1.00), warm median
  0.164 vs 0.157 s, attach 0.59-0.81 vs 0.58-0.85 s. Agent start, networking, browser
  view, sync and logins (6, 9, 13): D2n figures, not re-run.
- **Split.** Freeze (4): idle machines rest 7 of 7 (D1 never) and give their memory back,
  but the first touch of a rested machine costs ssh 5.50-5.56 s, attach 6.0-9.3 s and
  browser 8.1 s, against D1's 1.1-1.3 s on a machine that never rests (a 12 GiB restore
  takes 4.2-4.6 s; D2n's 6 GiB 1.5 s). Builds (7): H alone 40.6-47.9 s (D1 62.3-73.7), H
  three at once 52.9-59.2 s (D1 368-558, one stopped by the guard), P alone 25.5-30.0 s
  (D1 53.7-54.7), but **P two at once 53.9-205.4 s, slower every round (D1 68.3-76.9),
  and P three at once 774.9-811.2 s (D1 171-175)**, over 600 s on freshly booted guests,
  with the guests in the kernel 39-98 percent of the time. D2n (6 GiB, full-sync disk)
  did P three at once in 132-161 s. Cause unverified.
- **Worse.** Disk (14): a live grow is refused; a grow needs a stop, a resize and a
  start (~8 s and the machine's memory) where D1 grew live in 1 s.
- Worse rows inside better measures: `repose host restart` with three 12 GiB machines
  on 47 GB of free disk is refused (`no_room`), where D2n's 6 GiB machines fitted; three
  machines hold 22.2 GB after builds (D1's appliance 18 GB); nine machines on the Mac
  swap 8 GB where D1's guard would have stopped one.
- **Not compared.** Install, and the cold create: design2 still has no installer or
  base fetch.

Against the first D2n pass, every row it marked worse or not run: P alone now better
(25.5-30.0 vs 98-115 s), live resize no longer harmful (refused, nothing changes), a
killed VM comes back by itself (11.9 s), restore --as costs +10-17 MB instead of 4.4 GB,
the 61.5 s stop tail is gone, snapshot create on real data 11 s for 1.4 GB; still worse:
P two at once, and frozen first touch (worse than in D2n); newly worse: P three at once.

## Findings first (D2n final)

1. **Go builds in parallel collapse.** P three at once: 774.9, 808.9, 811.2 s in a clean
   run (only the three machines up, swap 0.19 GB, pressure normal), 437-704 s in the run
   with extra machines, and no finish in 600 s on freshly booted guests. P two at once
   gets slower every round: 54-62, 112-114, 202-205 s. P alone is fast (25.5-30.0 s)
   and H three at once is fast (52.9-59.2 s). In the guests: 0.5-4.5 percent user,
   39-98 percent system CPU, `/proc/pressure/cpu` some 60-72, no iowait, no swap; on the
   Mac the three VZ processes at 283-339 percent each, load 31, no thermal limit. The two
   things that changed since D2n's 132-161 s are the cached disk and the 12 GiB guests.
   Cause unverified; the checks to make are in measure 7.
2. **A rested machine's first touch costs 5.5-9.3 s.** hostd's restore of a 12 GiB
   machine takes 4.15-4.55 s even with a 263-276 MB image (7.65 s while another restores),
   against 1.54 s for D2n's 6 GiB machines. ssh 5.50-5.56 s (n=3), attach 6.03-9.27 s
   (n=3), browser view 8.10 s (n=1). Resting itself works: 7 of 7 idle machines rested
   at ~16 min, each VM process ended and its memory went back.
3. **The 12 GiB default makes the park need disk the Mac may not have.** `repose host
   restart` with three running machines was refused, nothing restarted, because the park
   reserves 12 GiB per machine below the 85 percent line and 47 GB was free (one fitted,
   two were `no_room`). With one machine it kept memory 3 of 3, back in 44-49 s. Disk use
   only grows: snapshots 13 GB (18:53Z) -> 32 GB (22:35Z) -> 44 GB (23:42Z), 39 kept
   clones; `repose rm` keeps a final snapshot and nothing in the CLI deletes one.
4. **More machines than memory: macOS swaps, nothing is stopped.** Nine machines up
   with three building (21:05-21:39Z): VZ footprints 32-35 GB on 24 GiB, swap to 8.0 GB,
   pressure level 2; `repose start` then took 47-130 s and a stop's snapshot 51 s. D1's
   guard would have stopped a machine instead. After a wake, `top` counts every woken
   machine's whole 12 GiB as its footprint (seven woken: 85 GB), held mostly as
   compressed zero pages (71 GB stored in 2.7 GB).
5. **What the slice-4 fixes did, measured.** P alone 25.5-30.0 s (D2n 98-115, D1 54);
   H alone 40.6-47.9 s; a live grow refused in 0.39 s with nothing changed (3 of 3), and
   snapshots restore after a stopped grow (the 40G one into the 70G machine in 4.4 s); a
   killed `repose-vm` boots again by itself, ssh in 11.90-11.94 s (n=3), and a fourth kill
   inside 10 min leaves it `error` with a message naming `repose start`; restore --as
   costs +10-17 MB and 5.1-5.6 s; the 60 s power-off tail did not recur in 55 stops.
6. **My mistake.** At 22:36 and 22:38Z a bad match in my new vm-kill script killed every
   `repose-vm` (eight machines of mine, nothing else was running). hostd brought all
   eight back in 90-110 s each time. It spoiled one freeze wait, which I re-ran.

Findings of the first D2n pass (f9d660b6), and where they stand: 1 (three busy
machines work) holds for H, not for P; 2 (P disk-bound) fixed; 3 (failed live resize
destroys snapshots) fixed; 4 (rest gives memory back) holds, the wake is slower; 5 (park
only through `repose host restart`) holds, now bounded by disk; 6 (killed launcher
stays down) fixed; 7 (61.5 s stop tail, full-copy restores) fixed. The first pass's full
text is in this file at 876e1a2c.
