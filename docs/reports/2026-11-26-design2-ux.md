# Design 2 UX on the Mac: D1 baseline and D2n, 2026-11-26

d2-lab (measurer) for the design-2 program (`~/briefs/design2-program-2026-11-26.md`).
Every number here was measured on one Mac, at the UTC times given, with the
instrument named. A number with no run behind it is not in this report.

Two passes, two sessions of d2-lab:

- **D1** (first session, 11:28-15:51Z): today's release on host `e2e-mac2`. Raw data
  `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab/data/`, scripts `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab/bin/`.
- **D2n** (second session, 17:06-19:25Z): the integrated design-2 skeleton on host
  `e2e-d2n`, after D1 was removed from the Mac. Raw data
  `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab2/data/`, scripts `/mnt/nixstore/sharing/d2-lab2/bin/`
  (the D1 scripts, with paths changed and the additions named below).

Each table has a D1 column, a D2n column and a verdict: **better**, **same** or
**worse**, with the numbers that decide it. "Same" means the ranges overlap and the
medians differ by less than about 15 percent.

## Findings first (D2n)

1. **Three busy machines work on D2n.** H three at once: 70.2-87.9 s, 9 of 9 rc 0, no
   machine stopped; D1: 367.8-558.3 s and the memory guard stopped 1 of 9. Three P at
   once: 132-161 s, 9 of 9; D1 171-175 s and 1 of 3 stopped. macOS's pressure level 1
   (normal), swap 0, compressor 0.6 GB throughout (18:06-18:18Z).
2. **The guest disk writes at 18-23 MB/s, and P alone is twice as slow as on D1.** P
   alone 98.3-114.7 s (D1 53.7-54.7), P two at once 98.6-118.3 s (D1 68.3-76.9). The same
   P with GOCACHE on `/dev/shm` in the same guest: 44.5 s (n=1, 18:2xZ), so P is
   disk-bound on D2n. `dd if=/dev/zero bs=1M count=1024 conv=fsync` to `~` in the guest:
   18.3, 18.4, 23.4 MB/s (n=3); macOS writes the same 1 GiB to the same APFS volume in
   1.60 s. Code read: `cmd/repose-vm/main.swift:374` attaches the data volume with
   `VZDiskImageStorageDeviceAttachment(..., cachingMode: .automatic,
   synchronizationMode: .full)`. Cause unverified; next check: the same dd with
   `.fsync` and `.none`.
3. **A failed live resize destroys the machine's snapshots.** `repose resize
   e2e-pocketmail 80G` on the running machine (17:37:47Z) exits 1 with `Could not resize
   e2e-pocketmail: cloud-hypervisor resize-disk: ch api vm.resize-disk: status 500:
   {"error":"resize of a running VM's ...` (100G and 120G the same, 3 of 3). The volume
   file is grown anyway: later resizes say `volumes only grow (invalid_argument)`,
   `lsblk` shows vdb 120G after a restart, while `repose status` still says `(40G
   disk)`. Every snapshot taken after that is of a 128849018880-byte device and every
   restore of one fails, in place 2 of 2 and `--as` 1 of 1: `restore stream: restore
   extents: extent 42949672960+65536 does not fit the 42949672960-byte device
   (snapshot of 128849018880 bytes)`. After the failed in-place restore e2e-pocketmail
   is `error`, `repose start` refuses (`has no usable volume because the restore into it
   did not finish`), and `repose rm` fails 2 of 2 (`lvcreate -s: clone g-01a21926-...:
   no such file or directory (internal)`), as it does for e2e-pm-as1, the failed
   `--as`. Both are still on the account. The user-facing text also names
   cloud-hypervisor and lvcreate, which a Mac does not run.
4. **Idle machines rest, and resting gives the memory back.** 3 of 3 machine-waits
   rested 15.7-15.8 min after the last touch (D1: 0 of 6 in 17-27 min). hostd rests by
   save: 5.6-6.5 s, a 1.25-1.58 GB image, the VM process ends, and its 6.16 GB footprint
   returns to macOS in that time (18:40:53-18:41:11Z). The first touch then pays the
   wake: ssh 2.63, 2.81 s, attach 3.17 s (D1, never frozen: 1.08-1.32 s).
5. **Park works through `repose host restart`, and only through it.** 3 of 3 restarts
   kept 9 of 9 machines' memory (same boot_id), command to all three answering ssh 56-67
   s. `sudo shutdown -r now` and the Apple menu restart (n=1 each) kept 0 of 3, as D1 did
   on every path.
6. **Killing a machine's launcher does not bring the machine back.** `kill -9` of
   e2e-recruiting's `repose-vm` (19:21Z): the machine went to `error` (`the machine
   stopped unexpectedly (hypervisor exited 0)`) and did not answer for the 141.9 s
   watched; `repose start` then had it on ssh in 5.6 s. D1's vfkit was relaunched the
   same second and its machines answered in 19-25 s. hostd itself is the opposite: `kill
   -9` of hostd 3 of 3 and a binary swap plus `launchctl kickstart -k` 3 of 3 left the
   machine running with the same boot_id, ssh in 0.79-3.93 s.
7. Smaller: two of nine stops with a snapshot took 61.5 s (hostd `guest did not power
   off; asking the hypervisor`, `power_off_ms` 60044 and 60035: the first stop of
   e2e-recruiting and e2e-unwrap after their create); the other seven took 3.7-4.9 s.
   Cause unverified (the first boot's background tool installs had finished by then).
   `repose rm` takes 11-34 s (D1 18-19 s), because the final snapshot is written in
   full. A restored machine's volume is a full copy (4.4 GB allocated each), not an APFS
   clone.
