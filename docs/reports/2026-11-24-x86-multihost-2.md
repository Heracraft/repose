## Method and what could not run

- **Timing and states.** Times are from `date +%s%N` around each command. States were polled every 3 to 5 s, with change-only logging.
- **Offline.** I made a box offline with `iptables -I OUTPUT -p udp --dport 51820 -j DROP`, with a `systemd-run` timer to undo it.
