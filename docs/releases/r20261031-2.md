## Why there are two releases on one day

`r20261031-1` shipped `wake-503` with a defect that CI found after both
hosts were switched: `Wait` read the stderr tail with no synchronisation
against the caller's reader, which `os/exec` forbids outright — "it is
thus incorrect to call Wait before all reads from the pipe have
completed". It reproduced 10 of 10 with a slow reader. The CI failure had
been seen before the cut and read as flake. It was not (I-926).
