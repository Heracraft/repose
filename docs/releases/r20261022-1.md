## The release.yml collision, and what it cost

`release.yml` FAILED on its `sign` job after the tag was pushed, and for
about ten minutes `v0.1.40` was a tag with no published release.

RELEASE.md offers two ways to get notes onto a release before the tag.
The first -- `gh release create <tag> --draft --notes-file` before the
push -- collides with GoReleaser: GoReleaser creates its OWN release for
the same tag, so there were two `v0.1.40` drafts, one with the notes and
no assets and one with five assets and an empty body. `gh release view
v0.1.40` resolved to the notes-only one and `sign` died on "no assets to
download". Recovered by moving the notes onto the release holding the
assets, deleting the empty duplicate, reattaching the tag (deleting one
draft untags the other) and re-running `sign`.

Recorded as I-860, which makes the second option the documented path.
